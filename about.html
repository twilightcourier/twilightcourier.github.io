I am a visual accompanist. My work features themes inspired by fantasy and broken technology. I usually do visuals for harder styles of music such as (Hardcore, Hardstyle, Gabber, J-core, Happy Hardcore, Speedcore, Electro, DnB).

When planning visuals for an event, I enjoy collaborating with event organizers to ensure that visual design is fully supporting the concept and energy of their event. I design visual themes that fit with the event's marketing as well as assisting with coordinating venue lighting, video projection, video mapping, and stage design.

I have mixed visuals at Club Kaiku, which is one of the most established nightclubs in Helsinki, frequently hosting international DJs. I was selected to participate in the national VJ competition in the city of Tampere. I have played at many underground parties, festivals, and events.
